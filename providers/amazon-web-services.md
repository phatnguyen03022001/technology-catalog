# Amazon Web Services

> **Provider SSOT.** Service claims and price evidence are recorded per offering below. `SOURCE_LINKED_SNAPSHOT` is not a current-price verification.

> **AWS Free Plan lifecycle (October 2026 signup experience):** The Free Plan is for experimenting and proof-of-concepts, applies only to eligible new AWS customers, and lasts up to 6 months or until credits are exhausted. Signup credits expire after 6 months; activity-earned credits have a 12-month expiry. After upgrading to Paid, remaining Free Tier credits expire 12 months after account creation. If Advanced Features are activated instead, already-earned credits expire 6 months after account creation. Check each grant in Billing because expiry differs by credit. Some services or offers are unavailable on the Free Plan. At expiry, AWS suspends the account and projects; data is retained for 90 days, then permanently erased unless the account is upgraded to restore it. Signups before the October 2026 experience use their original terms. [Official FAQ](https://aws.amazon.com/free/free-tier-faqs/).
